# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Welcome newcomers and different backgrounds. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
language, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads,
or restrict participation. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

Contact the repository owner privately about conduct concerns. Avoid posting
personal details in public reports. Vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
