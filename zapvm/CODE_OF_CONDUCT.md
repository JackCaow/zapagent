# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or level of participation.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, respect for
privacy and responsible disclosure, clear attribution, and a focus on evidence.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets,
and sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior threatens people, the community, or the
project's users. Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer.
Security vulnerabilities must use the process in `SECURITY.md`.

Enforcement decisions should protect reporter privacy and be proportionate to
the impact and context of the behavior.
